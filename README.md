# 🥷 THE CUL-DE-SAC SCRAP NINJAS: WORLD TOUR & ALIEN BREAKOUT

## 🎮 The Game

Three teen heroes defend their cul-de-sac with **Tank the Pitbull** (cyber-turret + loot sniffer) at their side. Blast Sleepwalkers across 5 worlds, dismantle props for scrap, collect the hidden Legend items, then duel the Legends 1-on-1 to unlock them for your squad.

### 👦 The Squad (customize all 3 in the Squad Lab)
- **JAX** — Lead Cul-de-Sac Striker
- **MARCUS** — Roof Turret & Tech Specialist
- **MATEO** — V8 Mechanic & Heavy Gunner

Customize name, gender style, hair color, eye color, and battle-cry voice pitch for each hero. Tag-team swap anytime with **[T]**.

## 🌎 The 5 Worlds + Finale
1. **Osceola, Arkansas** — Treehouse hub, junkyard, neon arcade, and the Blood-Moon Respawn Device **[B]**
2. **Memphis, Tennessee** — down I-55 to the Mississippi Riverboat base, Mecha-Catfish river barge, and the glowing Bass Pyramid
3. **Motor-Music City** — Nashville/Detroit hybrid: Music-Row and the V8 Vinyl Skyscraper Derby tower
4. **Caribbean Pirate-Jungle Island** — pirate stone fortress, volcano mud-bogs, and 6-foot monster-truck ramps under a swinging wrecking ball
5. **Area 51 / Retro Moon Base** — monster-truck cavern, zero-G UFO hangar, and the 100-Wave Portal Gauntlet
6. **Grand Finale: Mega-Siege** — the fused Treehouse + Riverboat Super-Fortress vs. Baron Broadcast

## 🌀 Modes
- **100-Wave Portal Raid** — every 10 waves the portal warps your squad to a new city. Build Bass Cannon, Tennis Turret, and Laser Gate defenses for 25 gold **[N]**.
- **Blood-Moon Respawn Device [B]** — respawns everything stronger at a higher Prestige Tier, with bigger XP/gold drops.
- **World Tour Super [Q]** — squad combo finisher.

## 🛠️ Tech
Single-file `index.html` — Three.js (WebGL, cel-shaded toon look with comic outlines), Tailwind, Web Audio API sound effects and voice lines, 1970s pulp-comic halftone overlay and comic action popups (BAM! KRAK!). Hosted free on GitHub Pages.
